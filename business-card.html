QUESTIONS

Smart business card FAQs

How do I start the free trial?

Choose a plan, create an account using your email and password, and open your Client Dashboard. The trial lasts seven days.

How do I pay after the trial?

Open the payment reminder in your Client Dashboard, select Pay Now, scan the Digital World QR code and submit your UPI transaction reference.

When does my five-year validity begin?

After the Digital World team manually verifies the submitted payment. Submitting a reference alone does not activate the card.

Can I use the digital card without an NFC card?

Yes. The Without NFC plan provides the digital business card and shareable profile link.

How do customers open my card?

Share your public card link. It is designed to open as a mobile-friendly business profile.
